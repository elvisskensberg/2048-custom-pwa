# Monopoly Deal — Official Rules Reference

Based on **Hasbro Official FAQ** and confirmed across US, UK, and Israeli (Kodkod/קודקוד) editions.
All three editions use identical gameplay rules — differences are cosmetic only (property names, currency symbols).

> **Sources:**

## Game Setup

- **Players:** 2–5
- **Deck:** 110 cards (shuffled)
- **Starting hand:** 5 cards each
- **Draw pile:** Remaining cards face-down in center
- **Win condition:** First player to collect **3 complete property sets** of different colors

---

## Turn Structure

1. **Draw 2 cards** from the draw pile (if hand is empty at start of turn, draw **5** instead)
2. **Play up to 3 cards** from hand (any combination of property, money, action)
3. **Discard** down to **7 cards** in hand (if over limit)
4. Turn passes to next player

### What Counts as a Play (1 of 3)
- Playing a property card from hand to table
- Playing a money/action card into your bank
- Playing an action card for its effect (rent, steal, etc.)
- Playing a House or Hotel from hand onto a complete set

### Free Actions (Do NOT Count as a Play)
- Rearranging property cards and wild cards between sets
- Moving Houses/Hotels between complete sets
- Playing Just Say No in response to an action

---

## Property Cards

### Standard Properties
- 10 color groups with varying set sizes (see Set Sizes below)
- Each property card belongs to exactly one color

### Wild Property Cards (Dual-Color)
- Can be placed as either of their two colors
- Value printed on card (1M–4M)
- Can be used for payment (goes to opponent's bank at face value)

### Property Wild Card (Rainbow / 10-Color)
- Can be placed as any color
- **Value: 0M** — cannot be used for payment
- Cannot be collected via Rent, Debt Collector, or It's My Birthday
- CAN be stolen via Sly Deal (from incomplete set) or Deal Breaker (from complete set)

## Wild Card Rearrangement

- Wild cards **CAN be moved between any sets** during your turn — **including out of complete sets**
- This is a **free action** (does not count as one of your 3 plays)
- You can rearrange **as many times as you want** during your turn
- **Constraint:** A complete set must contain at least **one standard (non-wild) property card** — a set of all wilds does not count
- Once your turn ends, card positions are locked until your next turn
- **Hasbro FAQ:** *"You can move wild cards and Property cards around as much as you like amongst the sets, but ONLY during your turn. Moving a card that is already on the table does not count as 'playing' a card."*

---

## Houses & Hotels

### Placement Rules
- **House:** Can only be placed on a **completed property set** (costs 1 play)
- **Hotel:** Can only be placed on a completed set that **already has a House** (costs 1 play)
- **Cannot** be placed on Railroads or Utilities
- **Maximum:** 1 House + 1 Hotel per set

### Movement Rules
- Houses and Hotels **CAN be moved** between complete sets during your turn
- This is a **free action** (does not count as one of your 3 plays)
- A Hotel still requires a House on the destination set

### Rent Bonus
- House: **+3M** to rent
- Hotel: **+4M** to rent
- Combined: **+7M** total (they stack, not replace)

### When a Set Breaks Up
- If a complete set is broken (e.g., property paid as debt), the House/Hotel becomes "orphaned"
- Orphaned buildings stay on the table and can be reattached to any new complete set during your turn (free action)
- Orphaned buildings **are vulnerable** to Sly Deal and Force Deal

---

## Action Cards

### Rent Cards
- **Standard Rent:** Charge rent for one of the two colors shown on the card (all opponents pay)
- **Wild Rent (any color):** Charge rent for any one color you have properties in (only one opponent pays)
- Rent amount = number of properties in that color group (see rent chart on cards)

### Double the Rent
- Must be played **with** a Rent card (uses 2 of your 3 plays)
- Doubles the rent amount for that charge
- Only **one** Double the Rent can be applied per rent action

### Deal Breaker
- Steal an **entire complete property set** from any opponent (including any House/Hotel on it)
- Can be blocked by Just Say No

### Sly Deal
- Steal **one property card** from any opponent
- **Cannot** steal from a complete set
- Can be blocked by Just Say No

### Forced Deal (Force Deal)
- **Swap** one of your properties for one of an opponent's properties
- **Cannot** take from a complete set
- **Can** give from your own complete set (breaking it)
- Can be blocked by Just Say No

### Debt Collector
- One opponent pays you **5M**
- Can be blocked by Just Say No

### It's My Birthday
- **All** opponents pay you **2M** each
- Each opponent can individually use Just Say No to block their own payment

### Pass Go
- Draw **2 cards** from the draw pile

---

## Just Say No

- Cancels **any action card** played against you
- **Does NOT count** as one of your 3 plays — can be used at any time
- Goes to discard pile after use
- **Can be chained:** A Just Say No can counter another Just Say No
  - Odd chain length → original action is **blocked**
  - Even chain length → original action **proceeds**
- Only **3 Just Say No cards** in the entire deck
- When used against multi-target actions (It's My Birthday), only blocks payment **for the player who played it**
- Can also be played into your bank as money (worth **4M**)

---

## Payment Rules

- Pay debts with cards **already on the table** (bank money, properties, action cards in bank, Houses/Hotels)
- **Cannot** pay with cards in your hand
- **No change given** — if you overpay, opponent keeps the surplus
- **You choose** what to pay with — opponent cannot dictate
- Rainbow wild (0M value) **cannot** be used for payment
- Properties used for payment go to opponent's property collection (they choose where to place them)
- Money/action cards used for payment go to opponent's bank

---

## Complete Set Protection

- Properties in a **complete set** are protected from Sly Deal and Forced Deal
- Only **Deal Breaker** can take properties from a complete set (takes the whole set + buildings)
- Houses/Hotels on complete sets are similarly protected (only taken via Deal Breaker)

---

## Winning

- First to collect **3 complete property sets** of **different colors** wins
- Complete sets cannot consist entirely of wild cards (at least 1 standard property required)
- Some rule printings state: **you can only win on your own turn**

## Common House Rules (Not Official)

These are **not** official rules but are commonly played variants:

1. **Deal Breaker nerf:** Can only be played if it gives you your 3rd complete set (winning move)
2. **Wild rearrangement cost:** Each wild card move costs "half a play"
3. **Win condition 4 sets:** Play to 4 complete sets instead of 3
4. **Hotel replaces House rent:** Hotel = 4M total (not stacking 3M+4M=7M)
5. **Just Say No turn cost:** Counts as a play if used on your own turn
