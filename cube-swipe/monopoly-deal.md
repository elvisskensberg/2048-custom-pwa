# Monopoly Deal — Game Rules

## Overview

Monopoly Deal is a fast-paced card game for 2–5 players based on the Monopoly board game. The objective is to be the first player to collect **3 complete property sets** of different colors.

## Setup

- Shuffle all 110 cards into a central **draw pile**.
- Deal **5 cards** face-down to each player.
- Players keep their hand hidden from opponents.

## Turn Structure

On your turn you must:

1. **Draw 2 cards** from the draw pile (mandatory, even if you have cards in hand).
2. **Play up to 3 cards** (optional). Cards can be played as:
   - A **property** to your field (building toward sets).
   - **Money** to your bank (face-up in front of you).
   - An **action card** to the center discard pile (apply its effect).
   - Any card (except buildings) can be placed in your bank as money, using the value printed in the corner.
3. If you have more than **7 cards** in your hand at end of turn, discard down to 7.

## Winning

The first player to have **3 complete property sets** on the table wins immediately — even during an opponent's turn (via a Just Say No chain, for example).

### Wild Property Cards (11 total)

- **Dual-color wilds** (10): Can count as either of two colors. Once placed, choose which set it belongs to. Can be moved between your own sets freely.
- **Rainbow wild** (1): Can be any color. Cannot be used alone to start a set.

### Rent Cards (13 total)

- **Dual-color rent** (10): Charge rent based on properties you own of one of the two colors shown.
- **Wild rent** (3): Charge rent for any one color group you own.

## Key Rules

### Paying Rent / Debts

- Pay with money from your bank and/or properties from your field.
- You choose which cards to give.
- **No change is given** — if you overpay, the opponent keeps the excess.
- If you cannot pay the full amount, you pay everything you have.
- **You cannot pay from your hand**, only from cards on the table.

### Buildings (House & Hotel)

- **House**: Place on a complete property set. Adds $3M to any rent charged for that set. Cannot be placed on Railroad or Utility sets.
- **Hotel**: Place on a complete set that already has a House. Adds $4M to rent on top of the House bonus.
- Buildings can be stolen or lost if the set is broken up.

### Just Say No

- Cancels any action card played against you (Deal Breaker, Sly Deal, Forced Deal, Rent, etc.).
- The original player can counter with their own Just Say No, and so on.
- Playing Just Say No counts as one of your 3 actions if played on your turn.

### Wild Property Cards

- Dual-color wilds can be placed in either color group.
- You can move wild cards between your own sets on your turn (this does not count as an action).
- Wild cards can be stolen by opponents via Sly Deal or Forced Deal.
- The rainbow wild (10-color) can represent any color but cannot start a set alone.

### End-of-Turn Hand Limit

- Maximum 7 cards in hand at end of turn.
- Discard extras to the center pile.

### Empty Draw Pile

- If the draw pile runs out, shuffle the discard pile to form a new draw pile.

## Quick Reference

- **Cards in deck:** 110
- **Starting hand:** 5 cards
- **Draw per turn:** 2 cards
- **Actions per turn:** up to 3
- **Hand limit:** 7 cards at end of turn
- **Win condition:** 3 complete property sets
